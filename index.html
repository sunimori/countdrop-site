3 COUNTDROP

A number puzzle
that cascades.

Drop a tile. Match the count. Watch it cascade.

One rule: a tile clears when its number equals how many tiles sit in its row or column. Simple to read, hard to master — every tile you drop reshapes the runs around it.

Download on the App Store Support

One board a day

Every player on earth gets the same tiles in the same order, with one attempt. Build a streak — and bank a freeze so a single missed day doesn't erase it.

Chains you hear coming

Clearing a tile cracks open the grays beside it. The sound climbs a scale as the cascade deepens, so you hear it building before the score catches up.

No timers, no rush

Turn-based and one-thumbed. Think as long as you like, then drop. Endless mode runs until the rising rows reach the top.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts.

Countdrop collects nothing, sends nothing, and asks for nothing. It works completely offline, and the whole game is there the moment you open it. Read the privacy policy — it is short, because there is nothing to say.
