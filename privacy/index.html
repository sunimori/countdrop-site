Privacy policy

Your game is yours.

Countdrop has no accounts, advertising, or third-party analytics. We operate no gameplay server and do not build a profile of how you play.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Information Countdrop collects

Countdrop does not collect personal information on a developer-operated server. The game contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK. Your game activity is not sent to us.

Where your progress is kept

Your scores, Daily Challenge history, streak, in-game currency, earned unlocks, and cosmetic selections are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, that progress is mirrored to your own iCloud key-value storage so another device can continue from it.

The iCloud copy is associated with your Apple Account and does not pass through a server operated by us. We cannot access or delete it. Sound and haptic preferences remain only on the device.

Deleting Countdrop removes the local copy. If iCloud sync was active, the iCloud copy can restore progress after reinstalling. If you do not use iCloud, deleting the app removes the only copy.

App Store purchases

Countdrop offers optional, non-consumable cosmetic purchases. Payments, purchase history, refunds, and your payment information are handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The app uses StoreKit on your device to request product information and confirm whether the Apple Account has a current verified entitlement. We do not receive your card details, and we do not copy App Store receipts to a developer-operated server.

Cosmetic selections may be included in your iCloud game save, but a selection alone never proves ownership. StoreKit verifies paid access again when the app launches or when you restore purchased items.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Countdrop submits scores and achievement progress to Apple’s Game Center service. Game Center can also provide a display name and profile image for features you choose to use inside the app. This data is handled by Apple; it is not sent to a server operated by us. Countdrop remains playable without Game Center.

Notifications

If allowed, Countdrop schedules a Daily Challenge reminder locally on your device. No push notification token is collected by us, and no Countdrop server sends the reminder.

Children

Countdrop is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy will be posted at this address with a new update date.

Questions about privacy?
We are happy to clarify how any part of Countdrop works.
